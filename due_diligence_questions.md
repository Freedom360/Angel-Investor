Core Checklist and Diligence Framework (From Girl Math Capital)

Core Checklist
Product: is the product excellent?
Brand: is the brand excellent?
Team: is this an all-star team?
Unit Economics: do the unit economics work? how about in retail?
Market/Opportunity to Disrupt: are we excited about this category and do we believe there are venture-scale returns to be had?
Traction: how quickly have they grown since launch, what retailer traction do they have?

Sample Diligence Questions
Company
Describe the company’s founding story.
Is there any past, present, or pending legal action against the company?
Has the company taken on any debt?
Team
List the management team and their backgrounds.
What prior startup experience does the team have, what role did each of them play, and what happened to the company?
What gaps or needs does the team have (Engineering, Sales, etc.)?
Who is on the Board of Directors (if anyone)?
Who are your Advisors (if anyone)?
Product and IP
Describe the product or service.
What is the product’s “secret sauce”?
Are there any patents associated with it?
What is the current status of the product or service’s development?
What future product or service developments do they plan to do? (Additional features, etc.)
Competition
Who are direct competitors?
Who are indirect competitors?
What is their competitive advantage?
Market
What is the target market, how big is the opportunity, and how fast is it growing?
What is changing in the market that makes this such a great opportunity for your company? (i.e., why now?)
What other markets do they plan to expand into, if any?
Go to Market Strategy
How do they intend to deliver the product or service to the market?
What is the pricing model?
What is your current and historical revenue?
What is the customer churn rate?
Exit Strategy
Who are possible acquirers?
Where do you need to be to get on their radar? ($10M in annual revenue, 1M users, etc.)
What are some recent acquisitions in this space?
What are typical acquisition multiples in this space?
Proposed Deal Terms
How much are they raising, and what are the terms of the raise?
How would you justify their valuation?
Breakdown of how they plan to use the funds.
What prior raises have they done? (Amount, terms, and who the investors were – Friends/Family, Venture, etc.)
If they plan to raise a future round, when, how much, and what would those funds be used for?
How much cash does the company currently have, and how long will it last you?
What is their current monthly burn rate?
Who is currently soft-circled to participate in this round?
Are pro-rata rights offered?
Documents
Annual financial statements (Income Statement, Cash Flow, and Balance Sheet)
Confirm no outstanding debt or accrued expenses owed to employees, founders, or shareholders
Financial model projected 18 months out
Copies of closing docs from prior rounds (if applicable)
